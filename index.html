Research

Ongoing · NLP

Fact, Inference, or Guess? A Gold-Labeled Benchmark for Evidence-Strength Discrimination in LLMs

I am investigating whether language models can reliably separate explicit statements, supported inferences, unsupported-but-plausible assumptions and contradictions in a source text, across eight sentence structures (temporal, causal, comparative, negation and others). The work builds a manually labeled dataset and fine-tunes a transformer classifier to benchmark against general-purpose LLMs, as a lens on hallucination.

Ongoing · HCI

Designing Inclusive Online Learning Platforms for Autistic Children in the Global South

Understanding compound accessibility challenges in Bangladesh. This project investigates the barriers autistic learners face in online learning environments and develops inclusive design implications for the Global South.

Ongoing · HCI and usable privacy

Understanding Digital Privacy and Security in Institutional Care in Bangladesh

Examines children's experiences of digital privacy and security in institutional care, and identifies inclusive design implications for shared digital technologies.

Accepted and Presented · GACLM

Early Detection of Anorexia in Social Media with BERT Embeddings

A BERT-based framework that combines contextualized language representations with multiple deep learning architectures (LSTM, BiLSTM, GRU and BiGRU) to detect anorexia early from social media text, with a comparative performance evaluation.
